🔥 HOW MARKETS WORK

Each Tournament Day runs 1:00 AM–11:00 PM ET with a fresh 20,000 credit Play Stack — nothing carries over between days. Predict UP or DOWN on fast, instantly-resolving markets.

Payouts are pari-mutuel, not a fixed multiplier — your payout depends on how the crowd split their credits on that market. Bet with the herd and win, and your multiplier shrinks toward 1×. Bet against a lopsided crowd and win, and it can pay out much bigger.

Your daily score is net credits won — everything you got back from settled predictions, minus everything you staked, that day.

⚔️ HOW BATTLE WORKS

The daily lineup is 8 All-Stars + 2 Underdogs. The two Underdogs battle for the full day — whichever gets more staked volume gets promoted to All-Star for the next day, and the lowest-volume All-Star gets dropped to make room. It's not just a trophy — it reshapes tomorrow's lineup.

📊 MOST WINDOWS DO NOTHING

One community analysis sampled thousands of sixty-second pump.fun price windows and found roughly four out of five were essentially flat — no real movement, just the last trade price carrying forward. Of the windows that did move meaningfully, direction skewed down more often than up.

The practical takeaway: most of the time you're not really predicting direction, you're predicting whether the market moves at all.

🎯 FOUR SEPARATE CONTESTS

Same free stack, same markets, same day — but four ways to place, and playing only one leaves the other three on the table.

🏆 Daily Leaderboard

Ranked by net credits won for the day. Deepest field, biggest total payout, lowest odds of finishing top spot.

⚙️ Grinder Quest

Most predictions placed that day, right or wrong. A volume contest, not an accuracy one.

💥 Smasher Quest

Highest single payout multiple from one correct prediction. Rewards a longshot, unpopular-side bet that hits.

🐣 Underdog Submission

No trading skill needed. Submit a token contract address; if it lands one of the day's two Underdog slots, that's a prize on its own, with a further bonus if it wins promotion.

🧠 STRATEGY TIPS
  • Payouts are pari-mutuel — the crowd's favorite pays less even when right. Look for lopsided markets and take the less-popular side when you have real conviction.
  • No day-to-day banking — credits reset daily, so consistency across days beats one lucky big day. Since nothing carries over, there's little reason to play conservatively just because yesterday went badly.
  • Underdog battles matter beyond the day — backing a token that gets promoted early means more exposure to it as an All-Star tomorrow.
  • Chasing Grinder Quest? Volume of predictions counts regardless of accuracy — many small plays beat a few big ones, and since it's pari-mutuel, small stakes cost little.
  • Chasing Smasher Quest? Multipliers are highest on the least-backed side of a thin market — stake small, take the unpopular side, and you only need to be right once.
  • Most windows are quiet — the edge is in spotting which ones are likely to actually move, not in guessing direction on windows that were always going to sit still.
  • Don't stake everything on one round — a single loss can wipe out several wins. Spread stakes across markets.
